LOCAL PREVIEW PRIVACY NOTICE

Your interest.
Your information.

This notice applies only to this local Revivex appliance preview. It is not a policy for the existing Revivex shop or any other product.

What is saved

If you submit the form, this computer stores your email address, your agreement to receive appliance development and early-release updates, the time of that agreement, and the form source. Duplicate addresses are stored once. No name, payment information or health information is requested.

Where it goes

The record is saved in a local SQLite database controlled by the preview operator. This preview does not send email, upload subscribers to another service, or share them with third parties. Registering interest does not reserve or purchase a product.

How the preview works

There are no analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. Fonts and product images are served locally. A short-lived form token and request-rate counters are kept in server memory to reduce automated abuse. Your network address is used temporarily for rate limiting, not saved in the signup database. Request-body and access logging are disabled.

Retention and withdrawal

Records remain on this computer until the preview operator deletes them. To withdraw consent or request deletion, contact the person who shared this local preview and provide the email address you registered. No automatic deletion or email-unsubscribe service is connected to this preview.

Before a public launch

A responsible business identity, privacy contact, retention policy and verified unsubscribe process must be provided before this page is published or any marketing email is sent. A separate authorization is required to connect email delivery or transfer these records.
