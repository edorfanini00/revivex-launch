FAQ

Good to know.

Can I buy the Revivex machine today?

Not yet. It's in development and nothing is for sale. The waitlist is how we'll share progress first.

Do I need a wearable?

No. It works from your goals, routine and Revivex results. Connecting Apple Health, Oura, WHOOP or Fitbit lets the app adjust your routine to how you actually slept, trained and recovered.

Does it use my biomarker results?

That's the idea. It's designed to work with the Revivex app, so your biomarker testing and daily data shape the routine the machine pours.

Who controls what goes in?

You do. The app suggests your routine within the limits of your plan, you can see exactly what's in it, and nothing changes outside those limits.

Does it need a water line?

No. It uses a removable tank that you fill at the tap and set back on top.

Do I mix anything in the glass?

No. Weighing, water and blending all happen inside the machine. The glass receives the finished drink.

Are these real photos?

No. Every image on this page is a concept visualization of a product in development. The final design may change.
