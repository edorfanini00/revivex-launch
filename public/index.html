Questions

What we can tell you today.

What does it make?

It is designed to make one drink each morning: your supplements, measured and blended with water, based on your data and your plan.

Do I need a wearable?

No. It is designed to work from your goals, routine and lab results. Connecting Apple Health, Oura, WHOOP or Fitbit is designed to let the app adjust your routine to how you actually slept, trained and recovered.

Does it use my lab results?

That is the idea. The Revivex app is designed to use the results you record in it, together with your daily data, to set what the machine pours.

Who controls what goes in?

You do. The app is designed to set your routine within the limits of your plan and to show you exactly what is in it.

Does it need a water line?

No. It is designed around a removable tank that you fill at the tap.
