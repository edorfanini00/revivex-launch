Questions

What does it make?

It is designed to make one drink each morning: your supplements, measured and blended with water, based on your data and your plan.

Can I order the Revivex machine today?

Not yet. It's in development, and the final price and shipping date have not been announced. Join the waitlist and we'll email you as we get closer to launch.

Do I need a wearable?

No. It's designed to work from your goals, routine and Revivex results. Connecting Apple Health, Oura, WHOOP or Fitbit is designed to let the app adjust your blend to how you actually slept, trained and recovered.

Does it use my biomarker results?

That's the idea. It's designed to work with the Revivex app, so your biomarker testing and daily data shape the blend the machine makes.

Who controls what goes in?

You do. The app is designed to set your blend within the limits of your plan and to show you exactly what's in it.

Does it need a water line?

No. It's designed around a removable tank that you fill at the tap and set back on top.

How does the waitlist work?

Joining is free and you don't pay anything. Leave your email address and country and we'll send you launch updates. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email, or by writing to support@revivex.store. See our privacy notice.

Are these real photos?

Not yet. The images show the design as it stands today. The final machine may change.
