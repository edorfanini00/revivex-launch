Questions

What does it make?

One drink each morning: your supplements, measured and blended with water, based on your data and your plan.

Can I buy the Revivex machine today?

Not yet. It's in development and nothing is for sale. The waitlist is how we'll share progress first.

Do I need a wearable?

No. It works from your goals, routine and Revivex results. Connecting Apple Health, Oura, WHOOP or Fitbit lets the app adjust your blend to how you actually slept, trained and recovered.

Does it use my biomarker results?

That's the idea. It's designed to work with the Revivex app, so your biomarker testing and daily data shape the blend the machine makes.

Who controls what goes in?

You do. The app sets your blend within the limits of your plan, you can see exactly what's in it, and nothing changes outside those limits.

Does it need a water line?

No. It uses a removable tank that you fill at the tap and set back on top.

Are these real photos?

Not yet. The images show the design as it stands today. The final machine may change.
