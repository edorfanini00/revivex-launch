Supplements only work if you take them. Revivex makes the taking effortless. Every morning, the same drink, prepared the same way, waiting in the glass.

How it works

Three steps. Two are yours.

Step one

Fill the tank.

A removable clear tank lifts out from the top. Fill it at the tap and set it back. No plumbing, no installation.

Step two

Choose your routine.

Pick a saved routine on the screen. Revivex weighs each ingredient, adds the water and blends inside the machine.

Step three

Take the glass.

The finished drink pours straight into your glass. Nothing to stir, nothing to rinse, nothing to measure.

Made for the counter,
not the cabinet.

The details

Quiet by design. Careful by default.

Blended inside

Mixing happens in the machine, not your glass. What pours out is ready to drink.

Two sizes

A standard 350 ml drink, or a concentrated 250 ml with the same ingredients.

Weighed, one by one

Each ingredient is dispensed and checked on its own before any water is added.

Stops when unsure

If anything doesn't check out, Revivex pauses and tells you, instead of guessing.

Questions

Good to know.

Can I buy Revivex today?

Not yet. Revivex is in development and nothing is for sale. The waitlist is how we'll share progress first.

Does it need a water line?

No. It uses a removable tank that you fill at the tap and set back on top.

Do I mix anything in the glass?

No. Weighing, water and blending all happen inside the machine. The glass just receives the finished drink.

Are these real photos?

No. Every image on this page is a concept visualization of a product in development. Final design may change.
